# Printify Canvas – Ready Files

All images in this folder are **Printify canvas-ready masters**.

## Specs
- **Pixels:** 4800 × 7200 (vertical 2:3)
- **DPI metadata:** 300
- **Format:** JPEG, sRGB, quality ~92
- **File size:** ~5 MB each (under Printify’s 100 MB limit)

## What sizes can you print?

| Canvas size (inches) | Effective DPI at 4800×7200 | Quality |
|----------------------|----------------------------|---------|
| 8 × 12               | 400                        | Excellent |
| 12 × 18              | 267                        | Excellent |
| 16 × 24              | 200                        | Excellent for canvas |
| 18 × 24*             | ~200 (slight crop or letterbox) | Excellent |
| 20 × 30              | 160                        | Very good (wall viewing) |
| 24 × 36              | 133                        | Good for large wall canvas |

\*18×24 is 3:4, not 2:3 — either crop slightly or leave a small border in Printify Product Creator.

**Best match sizes (exact 2:3):**  
8×12 · 12×18 · 16×24 · 20×30 · 24×36

## How to upload on Printify
1. Printify → **Catalog** → search **Canvas** (e.g. “Matte Canvas”, “Gallery Canvas”).
2. **Start designing** → upload file from `printify-canvas/christmas/` or `thanksgiving/`.
3. Fit / center the design (no need to stretch if you pick a 2:3 size).
4. Enable sizes you want; Printify will scale from this master.
5. Connect to Etsy / your shop and publish.

## Tips
- Prefer **2:3 canvas variants** so scripture text stays sharp and uncropped.
- Order one **sample** of the Santa / nativity / cabin designs before bulk listing.
- These files use accurate **King James Version** text (see `../KJV_VERSE_CHECK.md`).
- Full commercial rights — you own these for POD sale.

## Note on upscaling
Masters were generated at high quality then Lanczos-upscaled to 4800×7200 with light sharpening. Canvas texture is forgiving; at typical wall distance (3–6 ft) these print cleanly through 16×24 and larger.
